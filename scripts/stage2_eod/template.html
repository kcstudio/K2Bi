Plain-English guide

Short definitions for the words you will see as the desk grows.

Reading the research

Ticker
A short market code for a company or fund, such as G or SPY.
Watchlist
Investments K2Bi is checking. Being listed does not mean you own them.
SEC filing
A company report submitted to the US Securities and Exchange Commission. It contains public facts, not a live price.
Quote
A dated market price with its source. Historical prices do not imply current availability.
Freshness
How recent the underlying fact or price is. An old filing may be unusable for today’s decision.
Thesis
The evidence-backed reason K2Bi thinks an investment deserves attention.
Abstain
K2Bi withholds a decision when important information or a safety condition is missing.

Accounts and activity

Practice portfolio
A virtual record for testing ideas. It is separate from your broker account.
Intraday
An approach intended to open and close a position within one trading day.
IBKR paper account
Your broker’s practice account. It may hold paper shares even when K2Bi’s virtual portfolios are empty.
Position
Shares currently held in a particular account or practice book.
Order
An instruction to buy or sell. A proposal is only a suggestion until an order is actually submitted.
Fill
A completed transaction. Here virtual fills use a declared later-session close assumption.
Price limit
The highest price allowed on a buy order, or the lowest on a sell order. An order may remain unfilled.
Risk limit
A hard boundary on how much money or exposure a strategy may put at risk.

A simple reading order: Start with “No trade action from this research desk,” open Companies for the reason, then Trade guidance to see what a future proposal would contain. Practice portfolios and the IBKR paper account are separate views.
