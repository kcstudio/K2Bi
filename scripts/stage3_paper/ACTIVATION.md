# Stage 3 activation decision package

The local development package is prepared for review. It does not operate the paper account. Deployment, engine activation and the first paper order need separate approval and evidence. Stage 4 is the later 4 to 8 market-week observation period. Stage 5 live trading is separate.

## What is available

The desk displays distinct dated holdings, cash, account/order snapshots, historical closes, supplied research and source-byte checks. Refresh is manual. Rendering a saved file does not fetch data or make that file current. Every page uses an explicit fixed evaluation time.

The offline preparation adapter invokes the existing five validators for complete invented examples, with the existing read-only config. A rejection leaves later checks not run. It can call pure journal/broker reconciliation for complete supported invented records. Returned events, positions and orders are diagnostics only. Nothing is submitted, adopted or written. Recorded inputs remain not run because origin labels, hashes and supplied flags do not independently prove real coverage or approval.

The isolated payload comes from exact committed Git blobs, including the necessary pure rules and reconciliation dependencies. Its inventory can be verified against that same trusted revision. The old prepared version is retained separately. No private captures are packaged.

## Actual evidence and holds

The saved account shows a negative USD cash balance. This is a currency debit, not profit or loss. HKD and BASE totals do not establish spendable or settled USD. Settlement, restrictions, full reserved commitments and all manual/user order coverage remain unverified. The dated API-visible open-order snapshot is not guaranteed absence of every account order and is now stale.

The original holdings snapshot, newer cash/account snapshots, historical closing prices, source downloads and evaluation time have independent dates. None should be silently aligned or made fresh by rendering. The existing SPY holding is not adopted into a strategy book.

The bounded SEC downloads matched the expected company identities and saved report bytes. A recent download does not make the underlying filing or investment analysis recent. The neutral source note provides no verified buy/sell recommendation.

The sole quote probe failed locally before usable market-data evidence was obtained. Access, entitlement, observed feed and a current price usable for a trade remain unverified. The collector's local contract-ID repair was tested; it was not retried against the broker. No automatic retry is authorized by this document. Weekend, delayed, frozen, missing or unverified timestamps are not execution-price proof.

The offline recovery schema supports a strict subset of journal projections. Other known valid production events yield not run. Complete real journal/portfolio/order coverage and reconciliation must be independently established in the engine's existing recovery boundary. An empty or missing section is never assumed to be a real empty account.

## Proposed paper rules, not adopted

These are a decision draft, not a strategy approval or a config change:

- Paper account only; no live-account access. Cash-only USD orders, no autonomous FX or margin.
- Keep the current config unchanged: SPY, G and CDNS whitelist; maximum trade risk 1%, concentration 20% and aggregate open risk 5%. CALX and XLV are not allowed instruments. These maxima are limits, not recommended allocations.
- A candidate must have a separately approved current thesis, strategy revision and paper-trading rules. Old smoke-test approval does not authorize a new trade; stopped-out strategies remain stopped.
- Quantity and cost stay withheld until complete settled USD funding, restrictions, positions, all pending commitments, known stops and current marks are proven. No missing stop creates zero risk.
- Use a current permitted price with observed feed classification and usable source time during the permitted regular session. A historical close is never an executable quote.
- Run existing production validators and cash-only checks immediately before submission in the isolated engine. No desk approval substitutes for them. Maintain existing circuit breakers and human kill control unchanged.

## Proposed cadence and monitoring

Initially use a supervised, finite manual cycle during a regular US market session. Recheck account, orders and market evidence before any approved action. The adapter's 15-minute context expiry is an upper bound for an offline example, not a promise that a quote remains tradable for 15 minutes. The first operational cadence and quote age limits need explicit approval in the engine plan.

Record account/source/request identity, independent capture boundaries, server/source/receive times, config and strategy fingerprints, validator results and journal events. Review unknown, stale, mismatch, partial, connection and rejection states. Any uncertainty holds the action. Alert routing and unattended cadence are not activated by this package.

## Operator sequence and decision

1. Review the exact code revision, inventory, tests and runbook. Confirm the isolated destination and runtime dependency versions without overwriting the existing engine checkout or dirty baseline.
2. Resolve native settled USD funding, account restrictions, complete pending commitments and real journal/portfolio coverage. No currency conversion is proposed here.
3. Approve a current paper mandate and candidate strategy using the existing approval hooks. Obtain separately authorized usable current quotes and recommendation evidence. Do not repeat consumed capture allowances from this campaign.
4. Validate complete native USD risk context and reconcile the real journal/broker state using the existing production rules, with no mismatch override or orphan adoption. Confirm failure/restart behavior and human kill procedure.
5. Approve the exact isolated deployment, account, limits, cadence, monitoring, rollback and first supervised paper operation. These are external activation decisions, not local rendering actions.
6. Only then perform approved deployment and supervised paper operation through the engine. Keep readiness review and execution separate.

No step above has been performed merely because this file exists. The reviewable decision is whether to authorize that concrete activation sequence after the evidence holds are resolved. Full operational Stage 3 remains incomplete until verified paper operation succeeds.

## Rollback proposal

Keep the preceding isolated desk payload and its trusted inventory. If an approved future desk deployment fails integrity or offline checks, restore the preceding separate directory. Do not overwrite, restart or stop the existing engine as part of desk rollback. Engine recovery and trading shutdown stay under the existing operator runbook and human kill control. This proposal does not change either.
